## How

One grasp supplies everything: the loose minus firm depth is hardness, the firm
minus loose anisotropy is texture, and how much the can follows and sags under
the lift is the remaining load response. Take them all from that one sequence
([[one-grasp-multi-property]]).

## Traps

- **Do not require the pads to be symmetric.** Demanding similar marker
  displacement on both pads rejects almost every real hold, and on a can it
  throws away the low follow-through that is the measurement
  ([[grasp-verification]]).
- Do not require every channel to vote. Validity is defined by exactly two
  things: the loose close reading the 34 mm far plane, and the firm close
  showing contact clearly below it.
- Do not prepend the standalone weight recipe. This is one integrated
  measurement grasp, and the extra closes it would add are the firm squeeze
  that destroys the reading.
