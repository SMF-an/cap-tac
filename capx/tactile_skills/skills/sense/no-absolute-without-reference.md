## How

Ask what the number would be if nothing had changed, and whether you have that
value. If you do not, restate the judgement as a comparison inside the run:

- against a reference object, rather than against millimetres
  ([[lift-follow-height]]);
- two states inside one grasp ([[differential-across-states]]);
- two objects measured in the same session, rather than a threshold from
  another ([[no-calibration-transfer]]).

## Traps

- An absolute threshold tuned on one object is a reference in disguise, and it
  stops being valid the moment the object or the session changes.
- A difference answers "same or different" and never "which one". If the task
  wants an identity rather than a comparison, the difference is the wrong shape
  of answer ([[readability-not-identity]]).
- Ratios need a denominator that is actually measured. The texture recipe
  divides by the depth change between the two grips, and that denominator has
  to land where it was asked or the ratio is of nothing
  ([[texture-slope-two-grips]]).
