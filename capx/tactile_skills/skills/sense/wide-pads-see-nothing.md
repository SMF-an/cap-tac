## How

At full open the two gel faces are 80 mm apart, so a 60 mm ball sitting between
them keeps 10 mm clear of each gel.  The gel's description covers only what
touches it: with that clearance the pads describe nothing — not "smooth", just
no surface — even while the ball is squarely between them.

The pads come into contact only when the jaws close far enough.  A close is
therefore the first reading a between-the-pads ball gives.

The opening has a straddle width of its own: each jaw stands 40 mm off the
finger axis, so a 60 mm ball fits between the pads only while its centre is
within about 10 mm of that axis.  Further out the ball lies under the pad's
own body, still clear of the gel.

## Traps

- An empty description at full open is not evidence that the site is empty;
  it is evidence that the pads are wide.
- A descent onto a ball can run the whole way down with no reading; the change
  comes at the close, not from the height.
