## How

The ball rides about 34 mm below the pad centre.  At the carry height over the
bowl — pad centre 0.09-0.11 m — its underside is at 0.055-0.078 m, above the
rim's top at 0.051 m, and the bowl's mouth is 150 mm across against a 60 mm
ball.  Opening the gripper there drops the ball onto the bowl's floor with
nothing to clear; a descent only changes how far it falls.

When a descent over the bowl is refused, the refusal is not the delivery
failing: release where the hand stopped.  A hand that goes back to the box
with the ball still in the fingers is the one way this fails.

## Traps

- The descent is optional; the release is not.  Do not spend the budget
  re-finding the bowl.
- If the ball was held through a refused descent, it is still held: the
  refusal is the arm, not the grip.
